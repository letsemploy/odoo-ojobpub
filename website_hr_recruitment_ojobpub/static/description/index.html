oJobPub – Open Job Feed for Job Boards

Publish the open jobs of your Odoo website as an open, machine-readable feed that job boards and search engines can read directly from your website.

https://your-domain/.well-known/ojobpub.json

No job board accounts

Your jobs stay on your website. Job boards read the feed instead of you copying every job into several portals.

Works out of the box

Every published job position is listed with title, location, employment type, department and a plain-text summary. Expired and excluded jobs are left out.

Multilingual

Translated jobs get one entry per website language, with the matching language URL.

Setup

  1. Install the module. It depends on Website Recruitment.
  2. Open https://your-domain/.well-known/ojobpub.json to see the feed.
  3. Optionally register your domain at SourceTracker to get your jobs into the free daily export and API of Let's Employ.

Configuration

  • Website → Configuration → Settings → Job Feed: switch the feed on or off per website and choose which languages are listed.
  • Recruitment → Job Position → tab oJobPub: job type, work type (on-site, hybrid, remote), experience level, workload, salary range, keywords, an Apply Before date and an optional feed summary. All fields are optional.
  • Recruitment → Configuration → Employment Types: the oJobPub job type that new job positions get from their employment type. Odoo's standard types are mapped on install.

About oJobPub

oJobPub is an open, free format for publishing job openings on the employer's own website, similar to a sitemap for jobs. The schema is published under CC0 at github.com/letsemploy/schema. The feed contains only data you already publish on your job pages; the module sends nothing to third parties.
